Chatbot flow

Conversation design

1

Welcome

Greet the visitor and introduce the shopping assistant.

2

Ask category

Let users choose Shoes, Phones, or Laptops.

3

Ask budget

Offer three quick budget bands for simple decision-making.

4

Suggest products

Show 2-3 static products that match category and budget.

5

Choose next action

Users can view details or open a buy-now link in a new tab.

6

Collect email

Optional lead capture for follow-up recommendations.

7

Finish clearly

The bot closes the flow and provides a restart option so there are no dead ends.
